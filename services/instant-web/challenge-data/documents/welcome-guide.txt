Instant — Customer Welcome Guide
================================

Thanks for choosing Instant! This guide covers the basics of getting
started with your account.

1. Complete your account profile under Settings.
2. Explore the Products page to see available plans and add-ons.
3. Reach out any time through the Support tab — our team typically
   responds within one business day.
4. Generated reports (usage summaries, invoice recaps) are available
   under Documents and can be downloaded at any time.

Welcome aboard!
— The Instant Team
